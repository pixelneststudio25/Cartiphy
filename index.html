How it works

Two journeys, one platform

What a vendor does to open a store, and what a customer does to buy from one.

Sign up and accept the rules

Vendor creates an account and accepts the Marketplace Rules and Terms of Service.

Answer a few questions

Store name, category, single or multiple products, and a style preference. Cartiphy matches a template.

Add products

One by one, or by spreadsheet upload for larger catalogues.

Verify to get paid

Bank account and phone verification unlocks live payments and appearance in discovery.

Share the store link

Orders arrive in the dashboard and by SMS as they come in.

Deliver and get paid

Arrange delivery directly, mark the order out for delivery, and receive payment straight from Flutterwave.

Find a store

By direct link, or through Cartiphy's discovery page of verified sellers.

Browse and ask

Add to cart, or tap "Ask a question" to chat with the vendor on WhatsApp before buying.

Pay at checkout

Secure payment through Flutterwave. The vendor's contact appears right after, for delivery coordination.

Confirm delivery

One tap from an SMS link — or it auto-confirms after 72 hours.

Review or dispute

Leave a verified review, or raise a dispute within 7 days.

Trust in practice

How Cartiphy responds when rules are broken

A vendor rule is only real if breaking it has a visible, fair consequence. Circumvention (taking Cartiphy-originated buyers off-platform to dodge the fee) is handled on a graduated ladder. Genuine fraud skips straight to review.

01

Educational notice

First instance — a friendly, specific note on what to change.

02

Formal warning

Sent by email and kept as a record.

03

Removed from discovery

30 days, store keeps working, reinstated automatically.

04

Store suspended

No new orders; existing orders must still be fulfilled.

05

Permanent ban

Bank account and phone number blocked from re-registering.

FT

Fast track — fraud

Non-delivery, fake goods, or scam reports move straight to suspension pending review.

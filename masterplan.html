§1–2 · Product & locked decisions

Quick reference

Cartiphy is a Nigeria-first store-builder: vendors self-serve a store on their own subdomain from a curated template library; buyers pay through Flutterwave; shipping happens off-platform. Three roles — Vendor, Customer, Platform Admin.

§3 · Plans & entitlements

Prime, Venture and Apex

Annual billing is 10 months' price for 12. Trust and safety features — verification, reviews, disputes, buyer confirmation, buyer-facing messages, low-stock alerts — are never gated by plan.

Trial and lapse rules — everyone gets 14 days of Venture features (2% commission applies), starting only after Tier 1 verification, one trial per unique phone/bank account. After the trial or a lapsed subscription, the vendor falls back to Prime with all data intact; products over the Prime cap stay live but new ones can't be added. Upgrade prompts stay honest: live savings meter, moment-of-need cards, no manipulative modals.

§4 · Architecture

How a request becomes a paid, fulfilled order

Two flows: rendering a storefront, and settling a payment without Cartiphy ever holding vendor funds.

Request hits the wildcard subdomain

store.cartiphy.com — Next.js middleware reads the host header and resolves the store.

Template and data load

The matched template and the store's live data are loaded server-side.

Server fills the template

A Liquid/Handlebars-style engine fills placeholders and returns full HTML — so SEO and WhatsApp link previews render correctly, not an empty shell.

platform.js takes over

The shared client SDK handles cart actions, checkout initiation, and event tracking against the API.

Checkout total is built

Items plus the vendor's flat delivery fee.

Flutterwave split charge created

Commission routes to Cartiphy's main account; the vendor's share routes to their own sub-account. The seller bears the processing fee.

Order created only on verified webhook

Never on client-side confirmation — orders and order_items wait for a confirmed payment event.

Flutterwave settles directly

The vendor's sub-account pays out to their own bank account on Flutterwave's own schedule. Cartiphy never holds, releases, or gates this money — a direct consequence of no escrow.

Dashboard shows history, not a wallet

Earnings history is derived from orders/payments — never a "balance" or "available to withdraw" figure, since no such balance exists.

Security baseline — Phase 0, non-negotiable

  • Row Level Security on every table; vendor / customer / admin role model
  • Payment and webhook logic runs server-side only — service-role key never reaches the browser
  • Flutterwave webhook signature verification, idempotent via webhook_events
  • Rate limiting on auth, checkout, and confirmation-link endpoints
  • Automated cross-tenant test at every checkpoint: vendor A must fail to read vendor B's orders
  • Stock decrement is atomic — single transaction, rejected if stock is insufficient

§5 · Trust, safety & enforcement

Prevent first, punish second

A friendly block at the moment a rule would be broken stops most violations before they happen.

Always allowed

  • Answering buyer questions on WhatsApp
  • Coordinating delivery after payment
  • Selling to a vendor's own existing customers by chat

Prohibited

  • Phone numbers, bank details, or "DM to buy" in listings/store text
  • Directing a Cartiphy-originated buyer to pay outside checkout
  • Discouraging checkout use in any form

Verification tiers

TIER 0

Can build

Store creation only — cannot accept live payments.

TIER 1

Can sell

Bank account name-match + phone OTP. Appears in discovery, eligible for trial.

TIER 2

Verified Business

CAC-verified — earns the "Verified Business" badge.

Two enforcement tracks

Circumvention — graduated
01

Educational notice

Friendly, specific note on what to change.

02

Formal warning email

Kept as a record.

03

Removed from discovery

30 days, store keeps working.

04

Store suspended

No new orders; existing orders still fulfilled.

05

Permanent ban + blocklist

Bank account and phone blocked from re-registering.

Fraud — fast track
FT

Suspension pending review

Non-delivery, fake goods, or scam reports skip straight here.

Every action notifies the vendor with a reason and an appeal window. No money-based penalties — funds settle directly to vendors, and ToS must not claim payout holds as a penalty tool. The only sanctioned hold is the 24–48h bank-change security hold. Disputes: buyer may dispute within 7 days of delivery confirmation; unresolved after 5 days auto-flags the vendor. Refunds are vendor-initiated through Flutterwave, full amount only.

§6–7 · Data model & admin panel

Every table, grouped by domain

Money is integer kobo everywhere. Every table has created_at; every vendor-scoped table has store_id and an RLS policy. Search or filter by domain below.

Admin build stages

Built in stages, staff roles from day one

Roles exist from the start — Super Admin, Support, Moderator, Finance — even with a single user, so adding staff later needs no rewrite. Every action is logged in an immutable audit log.

§10–13 · Working rules & open items

Rules for every coding agent

    §11–12 · Tooling & start-now tasks

    Sessions, not tokens

    Codex runs via ChatGPT Plus — a usage-limited plan, not pay-per-token credits. Budgeting is about sessions and time. Task cards start modest and self-contained; reserve the most careful cards for Phases 0, 3, 4 and 8.

    Start now — long lead time

    • Flutterwave: confirm split payments/sub-accounts approved for a marketplace; confirm KYB docs
    • Termii: sender ID approval; WhatsApp Business API application
    • Resend: domain DNS (SPF/DKIM)
    • Vercel: paid plan; confirm wildcard-subdomain setup
    • Supabase: confirm backup coverage on the chosen plan
    • Legal (begins once live): vendor terms, Marketplace Rules, funds-model review, NDPA privacy policy

    Recurring costs to track

    • Vercel paid plan
    • Supabase paid tier (for backups)
    • SMS spend (Termii)
    • Domain renewal
